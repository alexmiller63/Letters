Valentine Rodriguez Gonzalez Jr. — Letter of Recommendation

Dear Tiffany,

I’d like to recommend Valentine Rodriguez Gonzalez Jr. for a position with the Santa Central team.

Valentine has no formal work experience, but he has qualities that I think would translate very well to this kind of work. He is remarkably even-tempered. He lives with people with very different personalities and backgrounds, including people who can sometimes be irrational or difficult, and he deals with them calmly. He is the one person in the house who seems to have no conflicts with anyone.

Valentine is also a very good troubleshooter. If something doesn’t work, he can usually figure out how to fix it. I’ve asked for his help numerous times. When a lamp arrived in pieces, Valentine figured out how to assemble it. When I couldn’t figure out the lanyard for my pulse oximeter, Valentine put it together.

That ability could be particularly useful at Santa Central. The camera has to be operated correctly and staff need to learn the procedure, but even when everything is being done properly, equipment can hiccup. Someone who is unusually good at troubleshooting may be able to get things working again quickly, potentially saving the company money and, more importantly, preventing a line of disappointed or angry guests who are waiting for their pictures.

I don’t know him well enough to make a sweeping claim that he is always responsible, but I have seen how he responds when something genuinely needs to be done. Recently, he needed to contact a government office and get an application sent to him. He understood that it was important, contacted them promptly, and got the process moving.

I haven’t seen Valentine working with children, so I can’t speak from direct experience about that. But based on how steadily he deals with adults with widely varying personalities and problems, I would expect him to be calm and patient with children as well.

Perhaps the strongest practical recommendation is that the owner and manager of the house frequently turn to Valentine when they need someone to run an errand or help with something that needs to be done. He has become, in effect, their go-to assistant.

Valentine would be starting his first job, so there will naturally be things he has to learn. But he is calm, adaptable, good at figuring things out, and accustomed to helping when something needs doing. I think he deserves the opportunity to show what he can do.

Santa Alex
