Tiffany,

When you asked me to recommend people for this year’s Santa team, Sarah was one of the first people who came to mind.

I didn’t want to write a typical recommendation letter. I’d rather just tell you about the Sarah I’ve come to know.

Sarah is rather quiet. In fact, she’s somewhat laconic. If you ask her how she’s doing, she’ll probably smile and say, “I’m fine,” and that’s about it. She isn’t someone who tries to be the center of attention, and she doesn’t use a lot of words. But I’ve learned that if you want to understand Sarah, don’t pay attention to how much she says. Pay attention to what she does.

One birthday, I happened to find Sarah walking home carrying an enormous potted flower arrangement. When she was younger, she had worked for a florist and had become remarkably good at creating beautiful arrangements. I asked her what it was for, and she simply smiled and said, “Happy birthday.” It wasn’t the flowers that stayed with me. It was the thought behind them. She had quietly invested her time and talent into making someone else’s day a little brighter, and she never made a big deal about it.

There’s another memory that has always stayed with me. Around adults, Sarah is usually laconic. But with our two cats, Gremlin and Elf, she became wonderfully talkative. She’d carry on gentle little conversations with them as though they understood every word. It was a glimpse into a side of her that many people might miss. Beneath that quiet exterior is someone who is patient, warm, and naturally nurturing.

Every Christmas season we have younger helpers, often sixteen-year-olds working one of their first jobs. They sometimes need help with things like paperwork or direct deposit, and I’ve often found myself thinking that Sarah would have been exactly the kind of person they would have been comfortable going to. She has a practical, common-sense way about her that makes people feel at ease instead of embarrassed.

That’s why I think Sarah would fit so well on a Santa team. Christmas isn’t really about the loudest personalities. It’s about making people feel welcome, noticed, and cared for. That’s something Sarah does naturally.

Santa Alex
